# Out Of Time

A 2.5D top-down action game made by team **Borrowed Time** for the **Bristol Game Jam**. Theme: **time**.

> **Time only moves when you move.** Your clock is your health. Stand still and the world freezes so you can plan, but the clock keeps ticking slowly. Get hit and you lose seconds. Run out and the level restarts.

Your time machine is broken. Travel through four eras, **Caveman → Medieval → Steampunk → Future**, fixing it in each one before your time runs out.

## How to play

| Input | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrow keys | Left stick |
| Sprint | Left Shift | Left stick press |
| Jump (land on enemies to stomp them) | Space | South button |
| Interact (talk, pull, pick up, craft, hit) | E | North button |

## Core mechanics

- **World time.** The world runs at your speed. Moving runs it at full speed. Standing still slows enemies, hazards, animations and the music almost to a halt, so you can stop and plan.
- **The clock is your health.** It sits in the top-left and drains while you play. It keeps draining at 10% speed while you stand still, so you can't plan forever. Sprinting moves you faster but burns time twice as fast.
- **Losing and gaining time.** Enemies and traps take seconds off the clock. Killing enemies, opening treasure and grabbing pickups give seconds back. At zero, the level restarts.
- **Objectives.** Each era has a short chain of objectives, shown in the top-right, that ends with repairing and boarding the time machine:
  - **Caveman:** gather wood and stone, craft a stone axe, deal with the boar, and recover three magnetic stones from the cave.
  - **Medieval:** get past the castle gate and its skeletons to the Time Crystal. Brave the trapped vault for the treasure if you dare.
  - **Steampunk:** repair the factory's gear tower to power the lift, then get past the Clockwork Guard to the first floor.
  - **Future:** the final era.
- **Interactables** have an outline: gold when usable, bright when selected, grey once used, red for enemies and cyan for crystals.
- **Personal bests** are the clock time you have left when you finish a level and a whole run, bonuses included.

## Building and running

### Requirements

- **Unity 6000.6.8f1**
- **Git LFS**

### Setup

1. Install Git LFS (`brew install git-lfs && git lfs install`), then clone the repo.
2. Open the project in Unity **6000.6.8f1**.
3. Import the **Undead Survivor** pack from the Asset Store (Window → Package Manager → My Assets). It's git-ignored because its licence doesn't allow redistribution. Without it, some characters and tiles are missing.
4. Open `Assets/Scenes/Caveman.unity` and press Play. The game runs through the eras in order from there.

### Making a build

1. Check **File → Build Profiles → Scene List** has the era scenes in play order: `Caveman`, `Medieval`, `Steampunk`, `Future`.
2. Pick your platform and click **Build**.

The Caveman, Steampunk and Future levels are generated by editor scripts under **Tools → Out Of Time**. To change one of those levels, edit its builder and rebuild rather than editing the scene by hand.
